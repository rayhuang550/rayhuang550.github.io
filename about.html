The story

My first research project was in summer 2023 at Stevens, getting a simulated drone to follow a moving ground robot. It used object detection, ROS and PID control together, and most of what I have done since has been in robotics.

I started electrical engineering at Boston University and transferred to Georgia Tech in fall 2026. In between, I spent summer 2026 at Stevens again working on imitation learning, and built PetVision, a pet feeder that won an IEEE Arduino contest. Now I work on tactile sensing for a humanoid robot in the LIDAR Lab.
